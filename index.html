🛡️ Landslide Safety Precautions
Follow these precautions when landslide risk increases.
01
Move to Safety
Move away from unstable slopes and follow official evacuation instructions.
02
Avoid Steep Slopes
Stay away from cliffs, steep hillsides and visibly unstable ground.
03
Avoid Heavy Rain Travel
During intense rainfall, avoid unnecessary travel through landslide-prone areas.
04
Keep Communication Ready
Keep your phone charged and watch official emergency warnings.
05
Never Cross Debris
Never walk or drive across an active landslide or debris flow.
