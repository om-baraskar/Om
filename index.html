🛡️ Landslide Safety Protocols

🏃

1. Move Away

If an emergency warning appears, move away from steep slopes, unstable ground and the landslide path.

🚫

2. Do Not Return

Do not go back into a danger area until responsible authorities declare the area safe.

📱

3. Follow Warnings

Follow official emergency instructions and evacuation directions from local authorities.

🌧️

4. Watch Heavy Rain

Heavy or prolonged rainfall can increase the possibility of slope instability. Stay alert when conditions worsen.

🏠

5. Reach a Safe Area

Move to a designated safe location, preferably away from slopes, gullies and drainage channels.

☎️

6. Emergency Help

If someone is in danger, contact local emergency services or responsible authorities.
