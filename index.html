🛡️ Landslide Safety Protocols

🏃

Move Away

If an alert is issued, move away from steep slopes, cliffs and unstable ground.

🌧️

During Heavy Rain

Stay away from landslide-prone slopes during prolonged or intense rainfall.

🛣️

Avoid Blocked Roads

Never approach debris or a newly collapsed section of road to inspect it.

📱

Follow Authorities

Follow evacuation instructions from local authorities and emergency services.

👨‍👩‍👧

Stay Together

Stay with your family, teachers or responsible adults and use the designated safe route.

⚠️

Never Return Early

Do not return to an affected area until authorities say that it is safe.

💙 Remember

Early warning + quick evacuation + safe decision-making can reduce risk.

This dashboard is a monitoring and educational system. Always follow official emergency instructions during a real event.
