🛡️ Landslide Safety Protocols

🏃

Move to Safety

If an alert is issued, calmly move toward the designated safe area.

⛰️

Avoid Slopes

Stay away from steep slopes, cliffs and unstable ground.

🌧️

Watch Heavy Rain

Heavy or prolonged rainfall can increase the risk of slope failure.

📢

Follow Warnings

Listen to local authorities, emergency services and evacuation instructions.

🚫

Do Not Return

Do not return to an affected area until authorities say it is safe.

📱

Stay Connected

Keep your phone available for emergency notifications and updates.
