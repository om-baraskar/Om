🛡️ Landslide Safety Protocols

🚨

1. Take the Warning Seriously

If the system shows a critical warning, stay calm and move toward a safer area.

🏃

2. Move Away From the Slope

Do not remain near unstable slopes, falling rocks, or areas showing signs of ground movement.

🛣️

3. Use a Safe Route

Follow marked evacuation routes and instructions from responsible authorities.

📱

4. Stay Informed

Watch official warnings and keep communication available during severe weather.

👨‍👩‍👧

5. Stay Together

Stay with family, classmates or your group whenever possible and help others follow the evacuation instructions.

⛰️

6. Avoid the Hazard Zone

Never return to an affected area until authorities say it is safe.

🌿 Remember These Safety Tips

  • 🌧️ Heavy or prolonged rainfall can increase landslide risk.
  • 📳 Sudden ground vibration or movement should be treated seriously.
  • 🌳 Watch for unusual changes around slopes and drainage areas.
  • 🚫 Never stand below an unstable slope during an emergency.
  • 🧭 Move toward stable, open ground when instructed to evacuate.
  • 📢 Always follow local emergency authorities over this demonstration system.
