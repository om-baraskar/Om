🛡️ Landslide Safety Protocols

🏃 1. Move Away

If authorities or the system indicate danger, move away from the landslide path and toward higher, stable ground.

⛰️ 2. Avoid Slopes

Stay away from steep slopes, cracks in the ground, falling rocks, loose soil and unstable structures.

🌊 3. Avoid Water Channels

Do not move toward rivers, streams or low-lying channels during heavy rain or a landslide warning.

📱 4. Follow Authorities

Follow instructions from local emergency services, disaster-management officials and your school or institution.

🎒 5. Keep Essential Items

If evacuation is ordered, take essential medicines, water, identification and a charged phone when it is safe to do so.

🚫 6. Never Return Early

Do not return to an affected area until authorities say that it is safe. Additional slope movement can occur after the first event.
