🛡️ Landslide Safety Protocols

🚨

If an Alert Appears

Stay calm and move away from unstable slopes. Follow official evacuation instructions.

🏔️

Stay Away From Slopes

Avoid standing below or directly beside steep or visibly unstable terrain.

🌊

Avoid Water Channels

During heavy rain, avoid streams, drainage channels and low-lying areas.

📱

Follow Official Alerts

Pay attention to local authorities, emergency announcements and evacuation orders.

🎒

Emergency Preparation

Keep essential items accessible and know the designated evacuation route beforehand.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Help Others Safely

Stay together where possible and help children and vulnerable people follow evacuation guidance.
