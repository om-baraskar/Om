🛡️ Landslide Safety Precautions
01 • Move to Safety
Move away from unstable slopes and follow official evacuation instructions.
02 • Avoid Steep Slopes
Stay away from cliffs and visibly unstable ground.
03 • Avoid Heavy-Rain Travel
Avoid unnecessary travel through landslide-prone areas during intense rainfall.
04 • Keep Communication Ready
Keep your phone charged and monitor official emergency warnings.
05 • Never Cross Debris
Never walk or drive across an active landslide or debris flow.
