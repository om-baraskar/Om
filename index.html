🛡️ Landslide Safety Protocols

🚨 1. If an Alert Appears

Stay calm and immediately follow your local evacuation plan or instructions from emergency authorities.

🏃 2. Move Away From the Slope

Move to a designated safe area away from unstable slopes, cliffs and the path of possible debris.

🌧️ 3. During Heavy Rain

Remain alert around steep slopes, drainage channels and areas known to have landslide risk.

🚫 4. Do Not Approach Debris

Never walk toward a landslide area to investigate it. Additional movement can occur after the first event.

📱 5. Stay Informed

Follow official emergency information and warnings. Keep your phone available for important instructions.

❤️ 6. Help Safely

Tell a responsible adult or emergency service if someone needs help. Do not enter an unstable area yourself.
