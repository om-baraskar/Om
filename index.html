💗 Landslide Safety Protocols

🏃 Move Early If an official warning is issued, leave the danger area promptly.
⛰️ Avoid Slopes Stay away from unstable slopes, cliffs and areas directly below them.
🌊 Avoid Flowing Water Never cross muddy flows, debris or rapidly moving water.
📱 Stay Informed Keep your phone available and follow official instructions.
👨‍👩‍👧 Stay Together Stay with your family or group and help others only when safe.
🛑 Do Not Return Early Do not return to the danger area until authorities say it is safe.
