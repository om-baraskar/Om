Security Directions

Important directions for responsible monitoring and response.

1

Monitor Alerts

Check sensor readings and warning conditions regularly. Unusual changes should be reviewed carefully.

2

Maintain Equipment

Keep sensors, wiring, power supply and network connections protected and properly maintained.

3

Follow Authorities

During an actual emergency, follow official directions from responsible authorities and emergency services.

🛡️ Landslide Safety Protocol

Stay informed
Monitor official weather and emergency warnings.
Take warnings seriously
Pay attention to official alerts and unusual environmental changes.
Use safe routes
If an official evacuation instruction is issued, follow designated safe routes.
Report dangerous conditions
Contact appropriate authorities when dangerous conditions are observed.
