Safety Precautions

  • Move away from slopes if cracks, falling rocks, or ground movement appear.
  • Do not approach an unstable slope to inspect the sensors.
  • During heavy rain, follow official local warnings and evacuation instructions.
  • Keep the sensor wiring and electronics protected from water.
  • Verify alerts with responsible adults and local authorities.
