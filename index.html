🛡️ Landslide Safety Protocols

🚨

Take Alerts Seriously

If the system reports danger, leave the hazardous area calmly and follow instructions from responsible authorities.

🏔️

Avoid Steep Slopes

Stay away from unstable slopes, cliffs and places where rocks or soil may fall.

🌊

Avoid Debris Flow

Never approach flowing mud, rocks or debris during heavy rainfall or a landslide warning.

📱

Stay Connected

Keep communication available and follow verified emergency instructions.

👨‍👩‍👧

Stay With Others

Do not wander into hazardous areas alone. Stay with family, teachers, staff or other responsible adults.

⛔

Do Not Return Early

After an alert, remain away from the affected area until responsible authorities say it is safe.
