🛡️ Landslide Safety Precautions
Follow these precautions when the monitoring system indicates increasing landslide risk.
01
Move to Safety
If dangerous conditions are announced, move away from the affected slope and follow official evacuation instructions.
02
Avoid Steep Slopes
Stay away from steep hillsides, cliffs, unstable ground and areas showing signs of slope movement.
03
Avoid Heavy Rain Travel
During intense rainfall, avoid unnecessary travel through areas known to have landslide risk.
04
Keep Communication Ready
Keep your phone charged and pay attention to official emergency warnings.
05
Never Cross Debris
Never walk or drive across an active landslide, debris flow or newly damaged road.
