🛡️ Landslide Safety Precautions
Follow these five precautions whenever the system reports increasing landslide risk.
01
Move to Safety
If HIGH DANGER is announced, move away from the affected slope and follow emergency instructions.
02
Avoid Steep Slopes
Stay away from steep hillsides, cliffs, unstable ground and areas showing signs of slope movement.
03
Avoid Heavy-Rain Travel
During intense rainfall, avoid unnecessary travel through areas known to have landslide risk.
04
Keep Communication Ready
Keep your phone charged and pay attention to official emergency warnings and evacuation instructions.
05
Never Cross Debris
Never attempt to walk or drive across an active landslide, debris flow or newly damaged road.
